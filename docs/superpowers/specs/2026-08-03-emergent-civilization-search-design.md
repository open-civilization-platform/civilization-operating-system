# Emergent Civilization Search Engine Design Specification (Issue #72)

## Objective
Build an automated analytics service and evaluation engine (`EmergentCivilizationSearchService`) to detect, catalog, and classify emerging societal archetypes and governance patterns generated by autonomous AI agents.

## Key Features & Component Design

### 1. Emergent Archetype Classification (`EmergentArchetype`)
- Classify civilizations into 4 primary emergent archetypes based on telemetry indicators:
  - **`TECHNOCRATIC_MESH`**: High Nexus node connectivity, high research velocity.
  - **`DECENTRALIZED_AGRARIAN`**: High food reserves, distributed regional claims, low industrial drift.
  - **`HIGH_VELOCITY_TRADER`**: High trade agreement count, rapid resource velocity.
  - **`SCIENTIFIC_DIRECTORATE`**: High constitutional rule proposals, high scientific validation scores.

### 2. Analytics Service (`EmergentCivilizationSearchService.java`)
- Analyzes active civilization metrics, trade frequency, Gini coefficient (resource distribution equity), and rule counts.
- Computes an `EmergenceScore` (0.0 - 100.0) measuring societal complexity.

### 3. GraphQL Query & Controller Integration
- Expose `@QueryMapping List<EmergentArchetypeReport> emergentArchetypes()` in `CivilizationGraphQLController.java`.
- Expose GraphQL query in `frontend/src/graphql/queries.ts`.
- Render Emergent Archetypes card on `frontend/src/pages/Dashboard.tsx`.
